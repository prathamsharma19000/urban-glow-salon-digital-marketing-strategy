# Urban Glow Salon – Digital Marketing Strategy (3 Month Plan)

## 📍 Project Overview

Urban Glow Salon is a local unisex salon operating for 1.5 years.  
This project presents a structured 3-month digital marketing strategy designed to increase bookings, improve online visibility, and strengthen brand trust.

Location: Punjab  
Business Type: Local Unisex Salon  
Marketing Duration: 3 Months  
Prepared By: Pratham Sharma  

---

## 🎯 Primary Business Goals

- Increase walk-ins and appointment bookings  
- Improve Google reviews and ratings  
- Build a strong Instagram presence  
- Enhance local brand visibility  

---

## 🧴 Services Offered

- Haircut & Styling  
- Hair Spa & Treatments  
- Hair Coloring  
- Facials & Grooming  
- Bridal & Party Makeup  

---

## 💰 Pricing Overview

- Haircut: ₹300–₹700  
- Hair Spa: ₹1,200–₹2,500  
- Facial: ₹800–₹2,000  

Targeted for middle-income customers.

---

## ⭐ Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

- Affordable premium experience  
- Personalized consultation  
- Hygienic environment  
- Friendly & professional staff  

---

## 👥 Target Audience

- Age: 18–45 years  
- Gender: Men & Women  
- Students & Working Professionals  
- Middle-income group  
- Local residents  

---

## ⚠ Customer Pain Points

- Expensive branded salons  
- Poor hygiene in local salons  
- Long waiting times  
- Lack of online trust & reviews  

---

## 📱 Digital Marketing Strategy

### Platforms Selected:
- Instagram  
- Google Business Profile  
- WhatsApp Marketing  

### Primary Channel:
Instagram (Reels, Before/After Transformations, Bridal Showcases)

---

## 📊 Content Strategy

### Content Pillars:
- Makeover transformations  
- Haircare tips  
- Client testimonials  
- Festive & seasonal offers  

### Posting Frequency:
- 3 posts per week  
- 2 reels per week  
- Daily stories  
- 1 testimonial weekly  

---

## 🔁 Lead Generation Flow

Instagram Content → DM/WhatsApp Inquiry → Appointment Booking → Salon Visit → Review Collection  

---

## 💸 Paid Marketing Plan

Monthly Budget: ₹15,000  

- Instagram Ads: ₹9,000  
- Content Creation: ₹3,000  
- Offer Promotions: ₹3,000  

Targeting: 5–7 km local radius  

---

## 🎁 Campaign Idea

**Glow Fest Offer**
- 20% off on Hair Spa  
- Free Eyebrow with Facial  
- Discounted Bridal Trial  

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 30% increase in bookings  
- 4.5+ Google rating  
- 3,000+ Instagram followers  
- 50+ monthly inquiries  

---

## 🏁 Expected Outcomes

- Higher walk-ins  
- Stronger online presence  
- Improved customer retention  
- Increased brand trust  

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Instagram Marketing  
- Google Business Profile  
- Canva (Design)  
- PowerPoint  
- WhatsApp Business  

---

## 📌 Conclusion

This 3-month digital marketing strategy focuses on:
Awareness → Engagement → Conversion  

By leveraging Instagram marketing and local SEO optimization, Urban Glow Salon can build a strong digital presence and achieve sustainable business growth.
